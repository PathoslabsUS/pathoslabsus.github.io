Automated market making for regulated event markets.

Pathos Labs builds and operates trading systems that quote continuous two-sided prices on regulated U.S. event-contract exchanges.

What we do

Market making

Two-sided liquidity across sports, economic and financial event contracts, priced from quantitative fair-value models.

Trading infrastructure

Low-latency exchange connectivity, order management and risk controls, written in Rust and Python.

Market data and research

Continuous recording of exchange and reference data, with replay tooling to test changes before they trade.

How we work

  • Regulated venues only. We trade on exchanges that operate under U.S. regulatory oversight.
  • Automation with limits. Every strategy runs inside hard position, loss and price limits.
  • Built on AWS. Our trading, recording and research systems run on Amazon Web Services.
